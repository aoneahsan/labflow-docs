# Moving to LabFlow

One page per product a laboratory or a patient might be coming from: what moves, what does not, and that product's prices as its own site stated them. Last checked 7 November 2026.

Read this first: LabFlow has no bulk import, of patients, results or anything else. A laboratory starts its catalogue and its patients fresh and keeps its old system as the archive for what came before. A patient cannot bring a record across at all.

To weigh the products against each other rather than move between them, use the [comparison pages on the LabFlow site](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/compare/).

## Coming from laboratory software

- [CrelioHealth](./creliohealth.md): A cloud LIS for diagnostic laboratories, priced in tiers for the US and Canada and separately for India, with onboarding charged on the assisted tiers.
- [LigoLab](./ligolab.md): An LIS and revenue-cycle platform sold in four tiers to pathology, molecular and reference laboratories, with no price on the page.
- [LabWare LIMS](./labware.md): An enterprise LIMS for laboratories of every industry, offered as LabWare SaaS, LabWare Hosted or on your own cloud, with a mobile app on the app stores.
- [LabVantage LIMS](./labvantage.md): A browser-based laboratory informatics platform (LIMS, ELN, LES, SDMS) sold as SaaS or on-premises licensing to research, QC and manufacturing laboratories.
- [STARLIMS](./starlims.md): A LIMS and lab informatics platform (LIMS, SDMS, LES, ELN, analytics) for research and development, quality control, public health and clinical diagnostics laboratories.
- [XiFin Empower LIS](./xifin-empower-lis.md): A SaaS LIS for high-volume, high-complexity, multi-specialty laboratories, sold beside XiFin's revenue-cycle product.
- [Clinisys Orchard Pathology](./clinisys-orchard.md): A web-based pathology module in the Clinisys range, for laboratories that want enterprise workflows and device interoperability in one system.
- [SENAITE LIMS](./senaite.md): An open-source LIMS under GPLv2 that you install yourself with Docker or from PyPI, or run with a service provider, with no licence fee.
- [OpenELIS Global](./openelis-global.md): An open-source, FHIR-native laboratory information system under the Mozilla Public License, deployed by ministries and implementers from single facilities to national networks.
- [CloudPOS Medical Laboratory Software](./cloudpos-laboratory.md): A Pakistani laboratory POS and management system for diagnostic and pathology labs, with billing, multi-branch control, WhatsApp and SMS report dispatch and FBR tax integration.
- [Labsoftware.pk](./labsoftware-pk.md): A cloud laboratory management system from Lahore with published rupee prices, report dispatch by SMS, email and WhatsApp, and a patient online report page opened by patient ID.
- [Nexus Pro LIMS](./nexus-pro.md): A modular LIMS from Pakistan for pathology laboratories and diagnostic centres, sold by licence purchase or monthly subscription, with separate patient and laboratory mobile apps.

## Coming from a patient records app

- [MyChart](./mychart.md): Epic's patient app: a person sees results, visits and bills from organisations that run Epic, and can view, download or send a copy of their record.
- [CommonHealth](./commonhealth.md): A public-service app from The Commons Project that collects a person's health data from several providers, stores it only on the device and shares it with the services the person trusts.
- [Patients Know Best](./patients-know-best.md): A UK patient-held record, free to the person and paid for by organisations, available on the web, on mobile and inside the NHS App, where the person chooses who to share with.
- [Andaman7](./andaman7.md): A mobile app that collects a person's health data from US hospitals and laboratories for free and lets them share it with a circle of trust, choosing what, with whom, and changing their mind later.
- [NHS App](./nhs-app.md): The NHS's own app in England: test results, GP letters and family or carer access, also usable through the NHS website without downloading anything.
- [My Health Record](./my-health-record.md): Australia's national record, signed into through myGov, holding pathology and imaging reports with the person deciding what is stored and who may access it.
- [OneRecord](./onerecord.md): An iOS and Android app that gathers lab results, immunisations and medications from different US health institutions into one record the person controls the sharing of.
- [Chughtai Lab App](./chughtai-lab-app.md): The patient app of a large Pakistani pathology chain: a profile with the complete history of that chain's lab reports, nearby centres and home sampling, on iOS and Android.
- [MY IDC APP](./my-idc-app.md): The patient app and online reporting system of Islamabad Diagnostic Centre, a Pakistani diagnostic chain with home sample collection.
- [Sehat Kahani](./sehat-kahani.md): A Pakistani telehealth app for doctor consultations from a desktop or phone that keeps the person's medical history, previous consultations, prescriptions and doctors' notes in one place.
