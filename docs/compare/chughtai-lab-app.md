# Moving from Chughtai Lab App to LabFlow

The patient app of a large Pakistani pathology chain: a profile with the complete history of that chain's lab reports, nearby centres and home sampling, on iOS and Android. **Last checked 7 November 2026.** <a href="https://chughtailab.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Chughtai Lab App yourself</a>, because a page that says something newer than this one is right.

The side-by-side comparison, with LabFlow's own limits, is [on the LabFlow site](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/compare/chughtai-lab-app/). This page is about making the move.

## Before you move

- Choose the Chughtai Lab App if your tests are done at Chughtai Lab, because its reports, home sampling and centre finder are all in that one app and the central laboratory is CAP accredited.
- Choose LabFlow if you use more than one laboratory, because the record belongs to you rather than to the chain, and a result from any LabFlow laboratory lands in the same account.

## The move, step by step

There is no way to bring a record across. LabFlow imports nothing from Chughtai Lab App or from any other system.

1. **Keep what Chughtai Lab App holds.** Download what it lets you download and keep it somewhere of your own. The pages we checked do not say how long it stays available after you leave.
2. **Find a laboratory that runs LabFlow.** The [directory](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/laboratories/) lists those that have published a listing. LabFlow's portal only holds results from laboratories on LabFlow.
3. **[Open the patient portal](../user-guide/patient-portal.md)** once a laboratory has linked your account to your record and released something.
4. **[Share a report, and stop sharing it](../user-guide/patient-portal.md#sharing-and-stopping-it)** when you choose. A share opens one whole report. There is no LabFlow ID to give a laboratory, no access request to accept and no sharing section by section.

## What each one says it does

"Not stated on the pages we checked" is not a no. Ask Chughtai Lab App. LabFlow's column lists only what has shipped.

| What we looked for | Chughtai Lab App | LabFlow |
|---|---|---|
| A price on the public page | No (<a href="https://chughtailab.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) | Yes |
| A free plan, or free for the person | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| The person decides who sees a record | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes. One frozen report at a time, by a link the person can withdraw. |

| Records from more than one laboratory or clinic in one place | Not stated on the pages we checked | In part. Every LabFlow laboratory the person used. Nothing is imported from outside LabFlow. |
| Records kept for years, stated on the page | Not stated on the pages we checked | In part. Free keeps 12 months, then read-only. The paid plans set no cap. No number of years is promised. |

## What LabFlow does not have

LabFlow has no ID a person can give a laboratory, no laboratory access requests and no sharing section by section. It has no desktop app, promises no retention period and makes no backup commitment. No laboratories run it in production. [Status and limits](../roadmap.md) has the full list.
