# Moving from Andaman7 to LabFlow

A mobile app that collects a person's health data from US hospitals and laboratories for free and lets them share it with a circle of trust, choosing what, with whom, and changing their mind later. **Last checked 7 November 2026.** <a href="https://www.andaman7.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check Andaman7 yourself</a>, because a page that says something newer than this one is right.

The side-by-side comparison, with LabFlow's own limits, is [on the LabFlow site](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/compare/andaman7/). This page is about making the move.

## Before you move

- Choose Andaman7 if your care is in the US, because it pulls from thousands of hospitals there and lets you decide what to share with whom and change it later.
- Choose LabFlow if your laboratories are not in that network, and you want the result to come from the laboratory's own release rather than a retrieval after the fact.

What you would give up, going by Andaman7's own pages:

- Share one section only (one test, one report). A share opens one whole report. There is no grant for the ECG only or the X-ray only.

## The move, step by step

There is no way to bring a record across. LabFlow imports nothing from Andaman7 or from any other system.

1. **Keep what Andaman7 holds.** Download what it lets you download and keep it somewhere of your own. The pages we checked do not say how long it stays available after you leave.
2. **Find a laboratory that runs LabFlow.** The [directory](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/laboratories/) lists those that have published a listing. LabFlow's portal only holds results from laboratories on LabFlow.
3. **[Open the patient portal](../user-guide/patient-portal.md)** once a laboratory has linked your account to your record and released something.
4. **[Share a report, and stop sharing it](../user-guide/patient-portal.md#sharing-and-stopping-it)** when you choose. A share opens one whole report. There is no LabFlow ID to give a laboratory, no access request to accept and no sharing section by section.

## What LabFlow does not have

LabFlow has no ID a person can give a laboratory, no laboratory access requests and no sharing section by section. It has no desktop app, promises no retention period and makes no backup commitment. No laboratories run it in production. [Status and limits](../roadmap.md) has the full list.
