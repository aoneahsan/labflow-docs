# Moving from CloudPOS Medical Laboratory Software to LabFlow

A Pakistani laboratory POS and management system for diagnostic and pathology labs, with billing, multi-branch control, WhatsApp and SMS report dispatch and FBR tax integration. **Last checked 7 November 2026.** <a href="https://cloudpos.pk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check CloudPOS Medical Laboratory Software yourself</a>, because a page that says something newer than this one is right.

The side-by-side comparison, with LabFlow's own limits, is [on the LabFlow site](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/compare/cloudpos-laboratory/). This page is about making the move.

## Before you move

- Choose CloudPOS if billing, FBR and provincial tax integration and WhatsApp report dispatch are what your Pakistani laboratory needs first, and a local vendor with a demo call suits you.
- Choose LabFlow if you want the clinical record itself: a release gate, quality control, custody of every specimen and a portal the patient keeps, with the price on the page and a free plan to start on.

What you would give up, going by CloudPOS Medical Laboratory Software's own pages:

- A backup commitment stated on the page. No backup or recovery commitment is made today.
- Reports sent by SMS or WhatsApp from the system. An SMS link opens on the handset. Nothing is sent from the system.

## The move, step by step

1. **Get your data out of CloudPOS Medical Laboratory Software first.** Ask CloudPOS Medical Laboratory Software how your data leaves it before you cancel anything; we have not checked that. Keep the copy: LabFlow reads none of it directly.
2. **[Create an account](../getting-started/quick-start.md).** Sign-in is with Google, and there is no password to set.
3. **[Set up the laboratory](../getting-started/create-or-join-a-laboratory.md).** Five steps, including its name, departments and time zone.
4. **[Build the test catalogue](../user-guide/test-catalogue.md)**, then [panels and reference ranges](../user-guide/panels-and-reference-ranges.md). These are typed in. There is no bulk import.
5. **[Invite your staff](../user-guide/users-and-access.md)** and give each person a role.
6. **[Add patients](../user-guide/patients.md)** as they arrive. There is no bulk import of patients or of past results, so your old system stays your archive for what came before.
7. **[Take orders](../user-guide/orders.md).** A system that sends HL7 v2 order messages can hand them over one at a time as files, each becoming a draft order, as [interoperability](../user-guide/interoperability.md) explains. There is no live feed.
8. **[Enter](../user-guide/result-entry.md) and [release](../user-guide/result-review-and-release.md) a result** on a real sample before you switch the bench over, and run both systems side by side until you trust it.

## What LabFlow does not have

LabFlow has no ID a person can give a laboratory, no laboratory access requests and no sharing section by section. It has no desktop app, promises no retention period and makes no backup commitment. No laboratories run it in production. [Status and limits](../roadmap.md) has the full list.
