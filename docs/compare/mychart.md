# Moving from MyChart to LabFlow

Epic's patient app: a person sees results, visits and bills from organisations that run Epic, and can view, download or send a copy of their record. **Last checked 7 November 2026.** <a href="https://www.mychart.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Check MyChart yourself</a>, because a page that says something newer than this one is right.

The side-by-side comparison, with LabFlow's own limits, is [on the LabFlow site](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/compare/mychart/). This page is about making the move.

## Before you move

- Choose MyChart if the places you get care run Epic, because the record then arrives on its own, across organisations, with one Epic ID.
- Choose LabFlow if your laboratories are independent ones that will never run a hospital EHR, and you want each result frozen, shareable by a link you can revoke, and kept in an account that is yours rather than the hospital's.

What you would give up, going by MyChart's own pages:

- Share one section only (one test, one report). A share opens one whole report. There is no grant for the ECG only or the X-ray only.

## The move, step by step

There is no way to bring a record across. LabFlow imports nothing from MyChart or from any other system.

1. **Keep what MyChart holds.** Download what it lets you download and keep it somewhere of your own. The pages we checked do not say how long it stays available after you leave.
2. **Find a laboratory that runs LabFlow.** The [directory](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/laboratories/) lists those that have published a listing. LabFlow's portal only holds results from laboratories on LabFlow.
3. **[Open the patient portal](../user-guide/patient-portal.md)** once a laboratory has linked your account to your record and released something.
4. **[Share a report, and stop sharing it](../user-guide/patient-portal.md#sharing-and-stopping-it)** when you choose. A share opens one whole report. There is no LabFlow ID to give a laboratory, no access request to accept and no sharing section by section.

## What each one says it does

"Not stated on the pages we checked" is not a no. Ask MyChart. LabFlow's column lists only what has shipped.

| What we looked for | MyChart | LabFlow |
|---|---|---|
| A price on the public page | No (<a href="https://www.mychart.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) | Yes |
| A free plan, or free for the person | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| The person decides who sees a record | Yes (<a href="https://www.mychart.com/Features" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) | Yes. One frozen report at a time, by a link the person can withdraw. |
| Share one section only (one test, one report) | In part (<a href="https://www.mychart.com/Features" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) | No. A share opens one whole report. There is no grant for the ECG only or the X-ray only. |
| A laboratory asks for access by the person's ID and the person accepts | Not stated on the pages we checked | No. Not in the product today. There is no ID to give and no request to accept. |
| Access can be withdrawn later | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Records from more than one laboratory or clinic in one place | Yes (<a href="https://www.mychart.com/Features" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) | In part. Every LabFlow laboratory the person used. Nothing is imported from outside LabFlow. |
| Records kept for years, stated on the page | Not stated on the pages we checked | In part. Free keeps 12 months, then read-only. The paid plans set no cap. No number of years is promised. |
| An Android app | In part (<a href="https://www.mychart.com/Features" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) | Yes |
| Works in a browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |


## What LabFlow does not have

LabFlow has no ID a person can give a laboratory, no laboratory access requests and no sharing section by section. It has no desktop app, promises no retention period and makes no backup commitment. No laboratories run it in production. [Status and limits](../roadmap.md) has the full list.
