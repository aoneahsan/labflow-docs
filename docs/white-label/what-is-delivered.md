# What is delivered

A licence covers the platforms paid for. LabFlow is licensed on the two it ships today.

## The web app

Every laboratory screen, the patient portal, the clinician portal and the public directory, at your domain. Your name, logo and colours are applied across all of them. Behind them are the same release gate, quality control and audit trail every LabFlow laboratory runs on; a licence does not switch any of them off.

## The Android and iOS app

The same product as a phone app under your name, built from the same code as the web app.

The iOS build is delivered when you provide or fund an Apple developer account. LabFlow itself is not published on the App Store.

Choosing both platforms costs less than the two apart. The [pricing page](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing/) shows the discount and the total.

## Not included

- **Source code.** Only a [buyout](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout/) hands it over.
- **Exclusivity.** Until LabFlow is bought outright, it may be licensed to other buyers too.
- **Your regulatory approvals.** A laboratory that uses LabFlow for patients needs its own accreditation, registrations and approvals where it operates. A licence supplies none of them.
- **Changes made only for you.** They are [custom work](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work/), quoted separately.
- **Costs you choose.** Your domain, your store developer accounts and any paid service you add are yours to pay.
