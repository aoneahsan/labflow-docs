# Updates and renewals

Every licence and every buyout includes product updates for a set number of months from the day it is paid. The number is shown at checkout and on the [updates page](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates/), read from our licensing service.

## What an update is

- **Fixes** to defects in LabFlow itself, deployed to your instance.
- **New releases** that ship in LabFlow during the period, applied with your branding kept.
- **Security and dependency upgrades**, on the same schedule as LabFlow's own deployment.

A change made only for you is not an update; it is [custom work](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work/).

## Renewing

A renewal extends updates for one licence. It is counted from the later of today and the day your current updates end, so renewing early loses nothing. Renew on the [updates page](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates/) with the licence key and the email address the licence was issued to.

## If you do not renew

Nothing stops. The deployment keeps running on the last version it received, with your records where they are. Renewing later starts a new period from that day.
