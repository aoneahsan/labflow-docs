# Page tours and How LabFlow works

LabFlow carries two guides of its own. A **page tour** explains the page you are on. **How LabFlow works** explains the product as a whole, one screen at a time.

## Page tours

Eight pages have a tour: the home page, the dashboard, Patients, Orders, Specimens, Results, Settings and Pricing.

- **The button.** *Take a tour of this page* sits directly before the theme control in the top bar, in the same place on every one of those pages.
- **The hint.** The first time you open a toured page, a quiet hint says *New here? Take a quick tour of this page.* with **Show me** and **Dismiss**. It appears once for each page — once on this device, and once for your account, so a second device does not ask again.
- **The steps.** Each step lifts one part of the page and says what it is for, as *Step n of total*. **Next** and **Back** move through it; **All steps** jumps to any step; **Skip tour** or the close button ends it at any point, as does Escape. The arrow keys move between steps.
- **On a phone** the step comes up as a sheet from the bottom of the screen, or from the top when the part it describes is low on the screen, so it never covers what it is explaining.
- **Every step has an address.** `?tour=3` opens the page's tour at step 3, so a step can be linked to directly.

The tour on the home page, for example, follows a specimen *from specimen to signed result*: six links — patient, order, specimen, test, result and report — and a reference range that is a function, not a column.

## How LabFlow works

[How LabFlow works](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com/how-labflow-works/) is a public page, reachable signed out, in six steps, each with a real screen and three short points:

1. **Register a patient** — a patient is a person, not a visit: one record each.
2. **Take an order** — orders come from your own staff or from a connected system.
3. **Accession the specimen** — scan or accession each tube as it arrives.
4. **Enter and release the result** — auto-validation suggests. A person releases.
5. **See what is waiting** — what is waiting and what is late, at a glance.
6. **Choose a plan** — start on the free plan, and pay by card when your laboratory needs more.

`?step=4` opens it at step 4. The arrow keys and, on a phone, a swipe move between steps.
