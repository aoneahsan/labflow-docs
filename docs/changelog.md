# Changelog

**The rebuilt LabFlow has been the live application at [labflow.aoneahsan.com](https://labflow.aoneahsan.com) since 24 August 2026.** Each entry below is one release of the web application, newest first. The Android app follows the same releases; its own status is on [The Android app](./getting-started/android-app).

## 27 September 2026 — email, speed and administration

- Every email moved onto one branded layout with a plain-text part, sent through one outbox. See [Email notifications](./user-guide/email-notifications).
- Public pages load from prerendered HTML, and the first screen asks for less code.
- Platform administration: plans and their limits, referral rewards, support access and the platform switches. See [Platform administration](./user-guide/platform-administration).

## 26 September 2026 — reporting, research and interoperability

- Saved report definitions, operational dashboards and rule-based findings that name their rule. See [Analytics](./user-guide/analytics).
- Research studies, consent and de-identified exports; population counts with small-cohort suppression. See [Research, population health and telemedicine](./user-guide/research-population-telemedicine).
- HL7 v2 and FHIR R4 exported and imported as files, and the slide viewer. See [Interoperability](./user-guide/interoperability).

## 25 September 2026 — the patient and clinician portals

- The patient portal: results in plain words, frozen reports, invoices, sharing and stopping it. See [The patient portal](./user-guide/patient-portal).
- The clinician portal, laboratory applications and My lab. See [The clinician portal](./user-guide/clinician-portal) and [Listing a laboratory and My lab](./user-guide/listing-and-my-lab).

## 24 August 2026 — the rebuild goes live

- The rebuilt application replaced the earlier one at the same address, with the same public URLs.
